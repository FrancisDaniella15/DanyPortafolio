## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide full-bleed website hero background, landscape 3:2 composition. Create an original dreamy botanical image inspired by soft out-of-focus tulips viewed from a low angle against a pale blue sky. Two or three very large YELLOW tulip blooms, buttery yellow to warm golden petals, soft organic green stems and leaves rising diagonally. Strong fine stippled film-grain / airbrushed pointillist texture across the whole image, diffuse edges, softly blurred shapes, luminous spring light. Artistic and tactile, restrained, not glossy 3D and not a sharp stock photograph. Composition for a portfolio hero: pale light-blue/ivory calm open sky throughout the upper left and central-left half, where dark HTML text will be overlaid later; place the main flower heads predominantly on the right third and foliage across the bottom edge. The sky must remain clean enough for large dark type, while retaining subtle grain. No text, no UI, no logos, no watermark, no people, no laptop. Flowers MUST be yellow, no red blossoms.
