## Corrección de referencia 3

Edit this typography comparison sheet, retaining the pure white background, black-only ink, the two-column structure, labels 3A and 3B, and the exact name in both options. Correct the lettering so ALL letters are unambiguously UPPERCASE: 'FRANCIS' on top, 'DANIELLA' below.
